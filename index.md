# KMSplit — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 10, 2026_

KMSplit ("the app") is made by KM Forge ("we", "us"). This policy explains what
information the app handles and why. We keep it simple because the app is simple.

## The short version
- We only collect what's needed to run the app: your name and email (from your
  sign-in) and the expense/split information you enter.
- We do **not** sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
- You can delete your account and data at any time from **Account → Delete Account**.
- You can use the app fully offline without an account; nothing leaves your device
  until you sign in to sync.

## What we collect and why
| Data | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Email address** | To create your account and identify you across groups | From Sign in with Apple or Google |
| **Name** | To display who you are in groups | You can edit it in Account → Edit Profile |
| **Groups, expenses, splits, settlements, notes** | This is the core of the app — tracking who owes whom | Stored so it can sync across your devices and shared groups |
| **Voice input (when you use the assistant)** | To let you add expenses by speaking | Speech is converted to text on your device using Apple's speech recognition. When the AI assistant is enabled, the resulting **text** (not audio) is sent to our AI provider to interpret your request. You can turn the AI off in Account. |

We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, health data, or payment card
information. KMSplit tracks balances only; it does not process real payments.

## Who processes data on our behalf
- **Supabase** — hosts our secure database and authentication. Your data is stored here.
- **OpenAI** — when the AI assistant is on, your typed/spoken request text is sent to
  OpenAI to interpret it. It is not used to train their models for this API usage.
- **Apple** and **Google** — provide the sign-in you choose; they authenticate you.

We never embed AI provider keys in the app; AI requests go through our secure server.

## Data sharing
Expense and balance information you enter in a **shared group** is visible to the
other members of that group — that's the point of splitting. Beyond that, we do not
share your personal data with anyone except the processors listed above.

## Retention and deletion
- Your data is kept while your account exists.
- **Delete your account** any time in **Account → Delete Account**. This permanently
  removes your account and profile. In shared groups, entries you were part of remain
  for the other members but are shown as "Deleted user" so their balances stay correct.
- Offline-only data lives solely on your device; deleting the app removes it.

## Children
KMSplit is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data
from them.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date reflects the latest version.
